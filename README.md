# 🍔 McDonald's Transaction & Sales Analytics

A deep-dive data analysis project exploring retail transaction data across multiple regional hubs. This project structures raw retail entries into actionable financial insights, evaluating product category mix, customer demographics, and store operational margins.

## 📊 Core Portfolio Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Transactions Processed:** 500 orders
* **Gross Revenue Generated:** ₹56,238 INR
* **Total Net Profit Captured:** ₹24,809 INR
* **Average Profit Margin per Order:** ~44.11%
* **Total Product Volume Demanded:** 990 units sold
* **Average Value per Transaction:** ₹112.48 INR

## 📈 Executive Business Insights & Summaries

### 1. High-Value Product Category Performance
Burgers remain the primary revenue engine for the business, while Beverages capture the highest volume cadence:
* **Burgers:** Captured ₹15,700 in Gross Sales across 110 items sold.
* **Beverages:** Generated ₹11,350 across a high-volume cadence of 210 units demanded.
* **Sides & Fries:** Combined for ₹19,258 in Gross Sales, demonstrating exceptional menu attachment rates.
* **Desserts:** Generated ₹9,930 in total transactional volume.

### 2. Demographic Purchasing Trends
* **Age Group Demand:** The `18–25` and `26–35` segments drive the highest order density, representing over 42% of total transaction volume.
* **Gender Breakdown:** Transaction density shows a balanced consumer footprint with Female consumers processing 48.2% of entries and Male consumers representing 47.4% (with 4.4% segmented as Other).

### 3. Operational Channel Efficiency
Delivery services dominate the logistical framework, while App usage leads payment processing:
* **Distribution Footprint:** `Delivery` leads operational fulfillment channels at 36.4%, followed closely by `Dine-in` (33.2%) and `Takeaway` (30.4%).
* **Payment Platform Mix:** Mobile `App` ecosystems process the highest digital entry layout at ₹20,150, outpacing physical `Card` transactions (₹17,840) and standard `Cash` (₹18,248).

## 🛠️ Data Architecture & Skills Demonstrated
* **Financial Metric Formulation:** Engineered column validation to track Total Price, Net Price, Cost Price, and explicit Profit generation.
* **Demographic Segmentation:** Categorized and aggregated macro consumer behaviors across multi-variable parameters.
* **Operational Logistical Modeling:** Evaluated store network productivity profiles across regional boundaries.
